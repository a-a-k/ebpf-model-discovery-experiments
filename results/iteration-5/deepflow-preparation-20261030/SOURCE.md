The [actual Bering graph](smoke-bering-model.json) has 17 services, 14 edges and
13 endpoints. The [actual command](smoke-bering-command.json) and
[accepted request sequence](smoke-accepted-requests.jsonl) preserve what Bering
consumed. This is a technical smoke, not an additional main measurement.
Its 24 source/operation forecast variants are unavailable, and Sheaft did not run.

The [separate AI reviewer receipt](real-smoke-review.json) identifies the retained
source controls, frozen inputs, actual model and external observations. It permits
the first already-planned failed-main repeat; it makes no positive usefulness,
accuracy or complete-cost claim. The equally informed direct baseline and all
preparation, failure, qualification and repetition costs remain part of the study.

The [saved-input review](saved-qualification-review.json) concerns engineering
equivalence only. Equality checks on its large remote qualification files were
executed by the reviewed Actions qualifier; those large files were not
independently inspected or recomputed locally.
Neither receipt claims local verification of a complete source ZIP. Their
selected-member verification limits remain unchanged.
