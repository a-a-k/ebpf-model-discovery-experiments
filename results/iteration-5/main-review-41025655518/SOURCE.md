measurement review, not a completed-study or positive-contribution claim.

[Actions evidence check 41025655518](https://github.com/a-a-k/ebpf-model-discovery-experiments/actions/runs/41025655518)

also inspected the selected source controls, actual graphs and actual simulator
models/commands where available. Each record states which bytes were inspected
locally and which checks ran in Actions. No whole local archive or complete worker
tree verification is implied. The separate reviewer is an AI agent, not an
external human peer reviewer.

The index binds original record size/SHA-256 and compressed size/SHA-256. Gzip
decompression restores the exact original record. Publication copies saved
records; it does not refit, simulate or recalculate any experimental result.

Unavailable forecasts are distinguished from absent graphs and from observed
application failures. No selection or rerun is driven by forecast availability
or accuracy.
All preparation, failed attempts, workers, review and publication costs remain
attributable; missing human effort and unfinished costs are not zero.
