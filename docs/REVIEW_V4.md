## Original criterion and strongest baseline

The contribution sought was the same useful independent application result with
less complete effort, or a better useful result at comparable complete effort.
The required path was documented Procrustes preparation, an actual Bering graph
from eBPF observations, actual Sheaft consumption where graph binding was supported,
and independent application outcomes after frozen forecasts. A plausible graph,
successful command, internal quality flag or product release was insufficient.

The strongest simple baseline receives the same mandatory-call contracts, identity
and placement inventory, conditional DB assumptions and intervention mixture.
Its exact controlled-service predicate is `A OR B`: the declared state weights give
0.875 for a mandatory two-replica service and 1.0 for an off-path operation.
All available primary graph forecasts equal the stored predictions of this
equal-information baseline. Monte Carlo does not improve equivalent arithmetic.
Supplied semantic knowledge cannot be credited as autonomous graph discovery.

Recovered evidence could still reduce preparation while preserving a useful result,
but that benefit requires comparable complete effort measurements. Human/agent
preparation was not measured. Runtime costs therefore cannot establish that claim.
No narrower demonstration replaces this criterion.

## Findings and limits

The evidence supports the actual eBPF graph-to-simulation path for Petclinic and
absent-mode OTel browse/cart. Other OTel eBPF forecasts and every DSB eBPF forecast
remain unknown. Petclinic's selected DB intervention uses recovered caller evidence
under supplied mapping and semantics. Coextensive whole-DB availability factors can
be mathematically equivalent; an outcome match alone does not prove every required
call was recovered. DSB's conditional DB assumptions and unsuccessful DB-fault
coverage remain explicit.

Operation classes do not establish autonomous business semantics. Exact parent
links, guest attribution and header corroboration are observed evidence, not
overall linking precision/recall: complete cross-guest ground truth is unavailable.
Native async association is separate from independently checked SQL persistence.
No superiority claim follows from graph size, class ARI or an internal admission.

The declared Actions snapshot covers 149 runs and 185 job rows, including failed
preparation, workers, CI, diagnostics and archives. The two then-live archives,
report's own duration and later publication work require the separately retained
closing metadata. Job durations overlap; Actions-artifact bytes are neither unique
storage nor total transfer bytes. Human/agent effort remains unknown, not zero.

The defensible conclusion is specific applicability with substantial limitations,
without demonstrated superiority or complete-effort advantage over the strongest
simple baseline. Completing this negative or unsupported finding answers the
original empirical question; it does not weaken the contribution criterion.
