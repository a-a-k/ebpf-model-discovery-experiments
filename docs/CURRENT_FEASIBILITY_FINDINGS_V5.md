# Current interpretation of the matched eBPF experiment

Both original actual-evidence reviews admit 79 of the fixed 81 main cases.
All 27 OBI, 27 Coroot and 25 DeepFlow cases are retained. Only DeepFlow/OTel
SDK-absent seeds 7/8 remain scientifically incomplete; their application workloads
have finished and processing/review remain. This is an interim
interpretation; the final full 81 comparison and figures remain due.

The question is whether the tested eBPF evidence can support operation graphs
and actual Sheaft forecasts when SDK traces are absent or incomplete. Availability
alone is insufficient: saved prospective models and commands must be bound to
independent external outcomes. Supplied operation contracts and placements are
available equally to every method, the direct conditional source-contract
baseline and the prespecified observed-edge union. No ML hypothesis, cost
superiority or new-calculation claim is required or inferred.

For OBI/OTel without SDK spans, forecasts are .875 and external outcomes are
2800/3200 for every operation/repetition except one checkout at 2799/3200.
This establishes a useful supported forecast in the missing-trace setting,
not superiority over the equally informed direct .875 baseline. OBI partial
OTel forecasts from the edge-union baseline retain adverse outcomes too; no
accuracy threshold selects cases. [OBI evidence](MAIN_EARLY_OBI_FINDINGS_V5.md).

The newly reviewed DeepFlow/OTel absent/6 case has no native SDK spans and all
24 operation forecasts remain UNKNOWN. Each of browse, cart and checkout
independently succeeds 2800/3200, matching the supplied conditional direct
baseline of .875. Thus missing forecasts persist even without the severe
checkout target degradation seen in some earlier repetitions. The exported
evidence and model pipeline do not reconstruct the required catalogue/RPC/DB
operation paths. Extra global database edges and the prespecified edge union
produce no additional forecast. This establishes a limitation of the tested
pipeline, not that every DeepFlow capture mode or eBPF implementation lacks
the information. [Absent/6 actual review](clean-study-otel-absent6-actual-review-20261104.json).

All 27 social-network cases are reviewed. Each tested eBPF pipeline lacks the
required operation-level framed binary Thrift RPC paths. Visible TCP, ingress
HTTP and Redis/MongoDB activity do not establish the missing RPC method/caller/
callee semantics. This is a limitation of the observed pinned pipeline, not a
claim that Thrift is undecodable. No specialized decoder was integrated or
tested here. HTTP acknowledgements also do not establish durable writes; the
pinned Lua compose receiver's exception-handling gap is documented with its
own-case causal limits. [Social-network evidence](MAIN_SOCIAL_NETWORK_LIMITATIONS_V5.md).

OTel runtime problems and missing graph evidence are separate findings. Own-case
kernel/container evidence establishes checkout memory-cgroup kills at 20 MiB in
DeepFlow full/6 and full/7. This invalidates an assumption of continuously healthy
nonintervened services, not the observed outcomes. It does not attribute every
failed request or prove DeepFlow caused the kills. Old Coroot restarts lack
proof of OOM. Causes are not transferred to other repetitions. Full/8 checkout
succeeds 2800/3200; partial/6/7/8 succeeds 1247/379/1029, all retained. Full/8 native,
combined and context forecasts equal the same conditional direct .875 baseline;
DeepFlow alone is unknown. [DeepFlow evidence](MAIN_DEEPFLOW_FINDINGS_V5.md),
[separate runtime evidence](MAIN_FIXED3_AND_OTEL_RUNTIME_V5.md).

Extra database edges are information, not automatically a useful operation or
database-fault forecast. Each selected forecast must have its own saved model,
command and report. All selected OTel database-down forecasts remain unknown in
the newly reviewed partial/full8 cases. Async 20 pending during a consumer pause
is not failure; later 20 exact persisted records show eventual persistence.
A shared explicit message ID across every causal stage remains unproved.

Technical timeouts, capacity-guard failures and publication failures are not
negative scientific outcomes. Complete valid UNKNOWN or adverse cases are not
repeated. All original and failed attempts, preparation, model work, overlapping
parent/worker waits, recovery, CI, review, transfer and finite retention/closing
costs stay attributable. Human preparation is unmeasured. The [current saved
evidence review](saved-capture-recovery-consumer-20261103.md) records original
capsule/compact identities and the limits of missing processed-full publications.
