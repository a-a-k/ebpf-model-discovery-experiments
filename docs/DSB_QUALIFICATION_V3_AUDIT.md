fields, frozen input and provided operation/identity knowledge. The proposed
diagnosis distinguishes parser coverage, observed identity/ancestry and binding
behavior. In particular, the generic unsupported-or-disagree message must be
checked against actual saved candidate bindings. Equal supplied semantics remain
available to all arms and the direct baseline; no superiority is claimed.

workflow remain unchanged. Actions verifies immutable API/ZIP identities, frozen
input and complete eBPF prefix, and copies original frozen models/entry/binding
JSON. Native files are copied with their hashes; their complete raw prefix is not
claimed to have been scanned. No traffic, fitting, replay, forecast, scoring or
admission is performed. Original capture settings and main81 scope are unchanged.

Preparation, CI, successful or failed diagnostic attempts and early stopping stay
in Actions history and the complete research cost boundary. Human review and
preparation time remain unmeasured. This inspection does not establish positive
research usefulness or give main81 GO; those require the separate actual review
of all 27 common-version qualification settings.
